The atelier

Three points

01

Infinite Colors

With our Infinite Colors service, you are able to create an exclusive and unique rug. Designed and produced to ensure a perfect harmony between design and space.

Every Corvo rug can be fully customized. Clients may choose from our Open Palette or develop bespoke tones through Infinite Colors. Sizes and shapes are tailored to each project, including non-standard and architectural formats.

02

Unmatched raw materials

In the world of high-end luxury rugs, the difference between good and extraordinary begins with material. At Corvo, we take time with this choice. Every fiber is selected for how it feels, how it wears over time, and how it lives in a space, not just how it looks.

We work with a wide range of exceptional fibers — Lyocell, New Zealand wool, natural wool, jute, and internally developed silks — each chosen for tactile quality, durability, and visual depth.

4–6 weeks

03

Production speed

All Corvo rugs are made to order in Portugal, produced specifically for the client. As a bespoke atelier, lead times typically range from 4 to 6 weeks depending on design complexity, materials, and destination.

Estimated timelines are confirmed at the time of order — craftsmanship and material quality, at a pace rarely offered in handmade luxury.

Rugs

Verdant

Inspired by an original watercolor painting by Ramiro Relvas, Verdant embodies the perfect union of artistic vision and masterful craftsmanship. The composition faithfully preserves the essence of the original artwork, reinterpreting its expressive color fields, organic forms, and evocation of the natural landscape through a refined and contemporary textile language.

Conceived for the most discerning luxury interiors, Verdant explores the richness of color, texture, and sculptural relief, transforming the fluidity of watercolor into an exceptional woven masterpiece. The interplay of varying pile heights, intricate weaving techniques, and meticulously crafted finishes creates remarkable depth, allowing light, texture, and volume to interact in perfect harmony.

Remaining true to the creative legacy of Ramiro Relvas, this rug preserves the distinctive artistic identity, sensitivity, and visual language that define his original illustrations, elevating them into a new dimension of design and exclusivity. Every detail reflects an uncompromising commitment to excellence, innovation, and artisanal savoir-faire, resulting in a timeless creation that transcends decoration to become a true work of textile art.

Verdant is the expression of an artistic legacy transformed into contemporary luxury — a unique creation where nature, color, and craftsmanship converge to enrich the world’s most sophisticated interiors.

  • Designer Tânia Resende
  • Produtor Sorema
  • Material Lyocell, Bamboo, Wool and Contract Lyocell

Rugs

Atlântico

The Atlântico rug is born from the essence of Corvo, a brand that forms part of an archipelago of luxury brands comprising Corvo, Graccioza, and Tercera — names inspired by the islands of the Azores, where nature reveals its strength, authenticity, and timeless beauty. Just as the islands that inspire their names, our brands are united by the Atlantic Ocean. It is the sea that surrounds them, defines their identity, and serves as their greatest source of inspiration: the serene power of nature, resilience through time, and enduring elegance.

The deep blue of the Atlantic, with its ever-changing shades and reflections, is expressed throughout this rug as a symbol of serenity, sophistication, and authenticity. Like the ocean that has shaped our islands, this piece has been created to stand the test of time, seamlessly combining tradition and innovation.

Wool is the defining material of this design. Natural, resilient, and exceptionally comfortable, it is one of the finest fibres available, valued for its durability, thermal insulation, and timeless beauty. To enhance the design with luminosity and depth, Atlântico also incorporates Lyocell, a naturally derived fibre distinguished by its subtle sheen and exceptionally silky touch. The combination of wool’s strength and the refined elegance of Lyocell creates a sophisticated interplay of textures and reflections, evoking the ever-changing surface of the ocean, where light is constantly transformed.

More than a rug, Atlântico is an expression of our identity — a tribute to the ocean that connects our brands, to the Azorean landscapes that inspire us, and to the excellence of Portuguese craftsmanship. A timeless creation designed to bring the tranquil strength of the Atlantic and the enduring sophistication of natural luxury into the world’s most refined interiors.

  • Designer Rui Santos
  • Produtor Sorema
  • Material Wool & Lyocell

Rugs

Lagoon

Inspired by the vastness of the Atlantic Ocean, this rug pays homage to the power, movement, and timeless beauty of the sea that surrounds the Azores islands. Just as the Atlantic shapes the landscape with its energy and serenity, this piece captures that duality through a play of textures, reliefs, and varying pile heights.

Every detail has been carefully designed to evoke the movement of the waves, the depth of the waters, and the richness of the forms that nature creates over time. The varying pile heights create a unique tactile experience, lending depth and dynamism to the rug, while the different textures subtly capture the light, revealing new details with every glance.

More than just a decorative element, this rug invites us to feel the tranquil power of the Atlantic beneath our feet. A piece that celebrates the connection between nature, craftsmanship, and contemporary design, transforming the essence of the ocean into an expression of living art.

  • Designer Rui Santos
  • Produtor Sorema
  • Material Lyocell
